# Classroom pilot kit
Consent: get permission from a teacher or guardian. Results are anonymous. Never record names, faces or voices of minors.

## Procedure (about 35 minutes)
1. Each student answers the 5-question quiz on paper (5 min). Use an ID number, not a name.
2. Students play Mission Closure for 20 minutes. Task: make a Mars design that closes.
3. Same quiz again (5 min). Note whether each student reached a closing design.
4. Record: class size, mean score before and after, number who reached a closing design. Report the real numbers.

## Quiz (answers at the end)
1. You add a heavier instrument but keep the same propellant. The spacecraft's delta-v: (a) rises (b) falls (c) stays the same.
2. Why does extra mass also need extra propellant? (a) Propellant is free (b) Propellant is also mass that must be lifted and accelerated (c) It does not.
3. A launch vehicle's capacity to low Earth orbit is 18,850 kg. Your fuelled spacecraft weighs 19,000 kg. Result: (a) closes (b) does not close.
4. The rocket equation links delta-v to: (a) engine efficiency (Isp) and the ratio of start mass to end mass (b) only the colour of the rocket (c) only the budget.
5. A number in a game says "REAL". What should you be able to do? (a) Nothing (b) Click it and see the NASA page and date it came from.

Answers: 1b, 2b, 3b, 4a, 5b.
